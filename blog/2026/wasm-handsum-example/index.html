Handsum Example

These album art images start as 147-byte Handsum 16×16 images (upscaled to 128×128, with a blur effect).

Click "Enhance!" to replace them with 256×256 JPEG images (downscaled to 128×128), which are typically 10,000 or 20,000 bytes. Even higher resolutions would obviously be even more bytes.

Click "Unhance!" to go back to the Handsum LQIPs (Low Quality Image Placeholders).

The 147-byte Handsum thumbnail images are small enough to inline into the HTML page (196 bytes with base64 encoding), minimizing network round-trips and improving page load time.
